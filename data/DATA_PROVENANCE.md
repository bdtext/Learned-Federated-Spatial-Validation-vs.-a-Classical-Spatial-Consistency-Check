# Data provenance, licences and processing

All observations are real station-level or airport-station records from national meteorological services, downloaded 14 October 2026 with the scripts in `code/dl_*.py`. Nothing is simulated except the injected faults, which are generated by `code/qc/inject.py` from fixed seeds and are fully reproducible.

## 1. DWD Climate Data Center (Germany): hourly station observations
- Source: https://opendata.dwd.de/climate_environment/CDC/observations_germany/climate/hourly/ (products `air_temperature` TU, `dew_point` TD, `wind` FF, `pressure` P0; `historical` files through 2025-12-31 and `recent` files through 2026-10-12).
- Licence: Creative Commons BY 4.0 (DWD terms of use, https://opendata.dwd.de/climate_environment/CDC/Terms_of_use.pdf). Attribution: "Source: Deutscher Wetterdienst".
- Selection (`code/dl_dwd.py`): stations inside the box 50.0-53.2 N, 6.0-9.6 E that started before 2023, are still reporting in October 2026 and have both historical and recent TU files: 92 stations (41 in North Rhine-Westphalia, 23 in Hesse, 16 in Lower Saxony, 9 in Rhineland-Palatinate, 2 in Bavaria, 1 in Bremen). Dew point available for all 92; wind and pressure for 37.
- Raw files: `data/raw/dwd/files/*.zip` (516 files, 373 MB) with SHA-256 checksums in `data/raw/dwd/download_manifest.json`.
- Processing (`code/prep_dwd.py`): hourly UTC timeline 2023-01-01 00:00 to 2026-10-12 23:00; DWD missing code -999 -> NaN; variables TEMP (TT_TU, degC), RH (RF_TU, %), DEWP (TD, degC), WSPM (F, m/s), PRES (P, sea-level hPa), PRES0 (P0, station hPa); DWD quality levels QN kept.

## 2. KNMI (Netherlands): hourly automatic weather station observations
- Source: KNMI script API https://www.daggegevens.knmi.nl/klimatologie/uurgegevens (all stations, per station and year; `code/dl_knmi.py`).
- Licence: Creative Commons BY 4.0 (KNMI open data).
- Variables: T (0.1 degC) -> TEMP; U (%) -> RH; P (0.1 hPa, sea level) -> PRES; FH (0.1 m/s, hourly mean) -> WSPM; TD (0.1 degC) -> DEWP. KNMI hour HH (1-24) is the hour ending at HH UT; mapped to the UTC hour HH (24 -> 00 next day).
- 51 stations returned by the API; 34 carry temperature for more than half of the period and are parsed (the others are precipitation-only or offshore platforms); 33 pass the 90 per cent temperature-coverage filter of the benchmark.
- Raw files: `data/raw/knmi/files/knmi_<stn>_<year>.csv` (204 files, 80 MB), checksums in `download_manifest.json`.

## 3. NOAA NCEI GHCNh (North America and Israel): hourly airport stations
- Source: https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/oa/global-historical-climatology-network/hourly/access/by-year/<year>/psv/ (`code/dl_ghcnh.py`); station list `ghcnh-station-list.txt`; the ISD station history `isd-history.csv` was used to map ICAO codes.
- Licence: NOAA/NCEI data are U.S. Government works in the public domain.
- Stations: the airports of the 36 cities used by the earlier study's NA36 corpus (27 US, 3 Canadian, 6 Israeli) were mapped to GHCNh identifiers; Ben Gurion (Tel Aviv) and Nahariyya have no GHCNh files for 2023-2026, so 34 stations are used.
- Of the 34 stations, 32 pass the 90 per cent temperature-coverage filter over 2023-2025. The GHCNh elevations of the three Canadian airports are missing in the station list (-999.9) and were taken from the ISD station history (Vancouver 3.0 m, Toronto 173.0 m, Montreal 32.0 m).
- Processing (`code/prep_ghcnh.py`): one observation per station-hour, the report closest to the top of the hour (ISD-Lite convention); variables temperature, relative_humidity, dew_point_temperature, wind_speed, sea_level_pressure, station_level_pressure and their NCEI quality codes.
- Raw files: `data/raw/ghcnh/files/GHCNh_<ID>_<year>.psv` (sizes 5-30 MB each; manifest with checksums).

## 4. Processed tables
- `data/processed/dwd_hourly.parquet`, `knmi_hourly.parquet`, `ghcnh_hourly.parquet` (long format: time, station, variables, flags) and `*_stations.csv` (id, name, state, lat, lon, elev, availability).
- Splits, DE/NL/EU: training 2023-01-01 to 2024-12-31; validation 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 (a full annual cycle); test 2026-01-01 to 2026-10-12. NA (GHCNh 2026 files are incomplete): training 2023-01-01 to 2024-06-30; validation 2024-07-01 to 2025-06-30; test 2025-07-01 to 2025-12-31.

## 5. What is and is not real
- Real: every observation, station coordinate and elevation, and (for GHCNh) every NCEI quality code.
- Synthetic: the injected faults (drift, step, spike, stuck-at, noise inflation), generated with the seeds recorded in `results/<corpus>/seed<k>/segments.csv` and audited in `audit.csv`.
